FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Explore clear and concise answers to the questions you care about most.

What services does EcoClean offer?

Eco Clean specializes in eco-friendly cleaning solutions for homes, offices, and commercial spaces. Our services include deep cleaning, carpet and upholstery cleaning, window washing, and more.

Are your cleaning products environmentally safe?

Yes, we use 100% eco-friendly and non-toxic cleaning products to ensure the safety of your family, pets, and the environment.

How much time does a typical cleaning session usually take to complete?

The duration depends on the size of the space and the type of cleaning service. On average, it takes 2-4 hours for a standard residential cleaning.

What should I do to prepare for the cleaning?

It’s helpful to declutter surfaces and floors so our team can clean more effectively. If specific areas need attention, let us know during booking.

Are your cleaning products safe for the environment and your family?

Yes, absolutely! We prioritize the safety of your family, pets, and the environment by using 100% eco-friendly and non-toxic cleaning products. Our solutions are carefully selected to deliver outstanding cleaning results without compromising on health or sustainability. Whether it's your home or workplace, you can trust us to maintain a green and healthy space.
